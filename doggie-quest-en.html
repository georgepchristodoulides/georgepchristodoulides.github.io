Doggie Quest

A six-level game for Android. The player guides a small dog, collects stars and travels through varied environments filled with enemies and obstacles.

The game

Six different levels

Doggie Quest combines platform movement, star collection and avoiding opponents. Its levels change both their appearance and mechanics, moving from tropical landscapes to aerial battles and snowy environments.

The game was designed for Android mobile devices and uses touchscreen controls displayed on the screen.

Installation: Android may request permission to install applications from the browser because the game is provided directly as an APK file.
