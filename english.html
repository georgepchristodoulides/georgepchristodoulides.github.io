Science books in clear language

Free Physics, Astronomy and Cosmology Books

George Christodoulides writes free educational science books that introduce modern physics and the Universe in accessible language.

Readers looking for physics books by George Christodoulides can explore introductions to astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology, medical physics, nuclear physics and condensed matter physics. Several titles are written especially for children, students and complete beginners, while Astronomy and Astrophysics provides a broader introduction for readers who want to go further.

The library

Physics and Astronomy Books by George Christodoulides

Free books on physics, astronomy, cosmology and related sciences, with dedicated pages, key topics and free access.

Astronomy for children

The Solar System for Children

An accessible introduction to the Sun, the planets, the Moon, asteroids, comets, telescopes and space missions. The book presents the Solar System in clear language, with images and scientific ideas that can be explored at home or in the classroom.

Cosmology for kids and beginners

Cosmology for Kids

A free introduction to cosmology for kids, children and beginners. It follows the history of the Universe from the Big Bang and cosmic inflation to the first atoms, stars and galaxies, and explains the cosmic web, dark matter, dark energy and observational evidence.

Physics and medicine

Medical Physics for Children

How is physics used to see inside the human body and treat disease? The book explains X-rays, CT and MRI scans, ultrasound, nuclear medicine and the principal technologies used in radiotherapy.

Modern physics for children

Nuclear Physics for Children

An introduction to the atomic nucleus, the forces that hold it together and the energy stored within matter. With clear explanations, the book presents radioactivity, fission, fusion, nuclear reactors and applications of radiation.

Materials and technology

Condensed Matter Physics for Children

The physics behind the materials and devices of everyday life. The book connects atoms and electrons with conductors, semiconductors, transistors, magnets, LEDs, batteries, solar cells and superconducting materials.

Astronomy and Astrophysics

A broader book about observational astronomy and the physics of the Universe. It is intended for readers who want to go beyond a first introduction and explore stars, galaxies and modern astrophysics in a systematic way.

The author

George Christodoulides

Physics teacher in public secondary education and author of free physics, astronomy and science books.

He teaches at Apostolos Pavlos Gymnasium in Paphos and joined the Cyprus Public Educational Service in 2009. He holds a BSc in Physics, an MSc in Medical Physics, an MSc in Astrophysics and an MPhil in Astrophysics.

His books aim to make important scientific ideas accessible to children and non-specialists, without hiding the real physics behind oversimplified explanations.
