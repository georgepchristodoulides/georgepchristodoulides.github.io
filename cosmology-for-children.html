An introduction to cosmology

A cosmology book for kids, children and beginners

Cosmology is the science of the Universe as a whole. It asks some of the biggest questions in science: How did the Universe begin? Why is space expanding? How did the first atoms, stars and galaxies form? What are dark matter and dark energy? And how can astronomers know what happened billions of years ago?

Cosmology for Kids introduces these ideas in clear language and with illustrations, without assuming that the reader already knows advanced physics or mathematics. It is designed as a first introduction to cosmology for young readers, school students and adults who are completely new to the subject.

The book does more than list facts about space. It follows the history of the Universe and connects modern cosmological ideas with the evidence behind them: the expansion of space, the cosmic microwave background, the abundance of the light elements, the distribution of galaxies and other astronomical observations.

Readers can therefore learn both what scientists think happened and why cosmologists think it happened. The aim is to make modern cosmology accessible while keeping the scientific story meaningful and accurate.

Questions explored

From the Big Bang to dark energy

The book builds a connected picture of the Universe rather than treating each topic as an isolated fact.

  • What is the Big Bang, and what does it mean for the Universe to expand?
  • What happened during cosmic inflation?
  • How did the first elements and atoms form?
  • What is the cosmic microwave background?
  • How did stars, galaxies and the cosmic web develop?
  • What evidence do we have for dark matter?
  • What is dark energy, and why is expansion accelerating?
  • How do astronomical observations test cosmological ideas?
